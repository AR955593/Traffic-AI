Privacy Policy & Legal Disclosures

Data Processing, Geolocation Policies and Licensing
Open Full Document

1. Precise Location & Geolocation Privacy

TrafficAI accesses your device's geolocation strictly during active route planning and live map viewing. GPS coordinates are processed transiently to center maps and calculate turn-by-turn road routes. Coordinates are transmitted securely over TLS 1.3 encrypted HTTPS. Persistent GPS tracklogs are never retained, sold, or shared with advertisers.

2. Live Traffic & Third-Party Telemetry

Real-time traffic flow data and road incident feeds are requested securely from TomTom NV and OpenWeatherMap APIs solely for calculation of road delays. Weather data requests are ephemeral based on map center coordinates.

3. Data Deletion Rights

You may permanently clear all personal saved places, saved routes, and commute logs at any time via the Profile screen or Settings. Account deletion requests permanently remove all records from the SQLite database.

4. Open Source & Data Attributions

© 2026 TrafficAI. Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors & TomTom NV. Weather data © OpenWeatherMap.
